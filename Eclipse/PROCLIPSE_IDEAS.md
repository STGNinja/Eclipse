# Proclipse: Your Proactive Intelligence

**Proclipse** is more than an AI—it's your Executive Assistant. It doesn't just wait for commands; it anticipates your needs, manages your time, and connects the dots across your digital life.

## 🧠 Core Philosophy: "Don't Ask, Anticipate"

### 1. The "Morning Briefing" Protocol
Every morning, Proclipse wakes up before you do.
*   **Feature**: When you open the app, Proclipse presents a "Daily Digest" card.
*   **Content**: "Good morning, Jaxon. You have 3 meetings today. Your 2 PM is with the design team, so I've pulled up the latest Figma links and your notes from last week. Also, it's going to rain, so take an umbrella."

### 2. Contextual "Echo"
Proclipse remembers the *small* things so you don't have to.
*   **Scenario**: User says, "Remind me to call Sarah later."
*   **Proclipse Action**: Checks your contacts for "Sarah", checks your calendar for free time in the evening, and sets a tentative reminder for 6 PM.
*   **Later**: "It's 6 PM and you're free. Shall I dial Sarah now?"

### 3. Smart Calendar Guardian
Proclipse actively defends your time.
*   **Scenario**: You get an email invite for a meeting at 4 PM.
*   **Proclipse Action**: "You usually work out at 4 PM on Tuesdays. Do you want me to accept this, or suggest 5 PM instead?"

### 4. The "Preparation" Engine
Proclipse detects upcoming events and prepares the necessary context.
*   **Scenario**: "Flight to NYC" is on your calendar for tomorrow.
*   **Proclipse Action**: Automatically creates a "Travel Pack" in your artifacts.
    *   Target Weather (NYC)
    *   Uber estimate to airport
    *   Boarding pass screenshot (retrieved from email)
    *   "Don't forget your charger" reminder.

## 💡 Example Proactive Interactions

### 📅 Time & Schedule
*   "I noticed you have back-to-back meetings from 1 PM to 4 PM. I've blocked off 12:30 PM for lunch so you don't forget to eat."
*   "Your next meeting is a Zoom call. I've tested your microphone and it looks good."

### 🔗 Contextual Recall
*   **User**: "What was that book Mike mentioned?"
*   **Proclipse**: "Two weeks ago, Mike mentioned 'Project Hail Mary' in the 'Book Club' chat. Want me to find it on Audible?"

### ⚠️ Intelligent Nudges
*   "You've been coding for 4 hours straight. Your productivity usually dips after 3. How about a 10-minute walk?"
*   "You're drafting an email to the CEO. You sound a bit angry. Want me to soften the tone?"

### ⚡ Automated Workflows
*   **"Focus Mode"**: When you start a coding session, Proclipse automatically silences Slack notifications and puts your phone on DND.
*   **"Meeting Wrap-up"**: After a calendar event ends, Proclipse asks: "Any action items from that meeting? I can draft the follow-up email."
